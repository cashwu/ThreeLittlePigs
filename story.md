## p.2

Once upon a time,
a little ant lived in a beautiful meadow.
All summer long, the grass grew tall,
and the flowers were bright and colorful.
The ant spent every day gathering seeds
for her family in the meadow.
She worked hard, and was always busy.

## p.3

One sunny morning, she found a golden seed
beside a patch of clover.
It was heavy, but she lifted it
and carried it toward her home.
A grasshopper watched her
from the top of a bending leaf.
He sang a cheerful song
while she hurried through the long grass.

## p.4

The next day, the ant returned
to gather more food.
The grasshopper was still singing,
dancing, and enjoying the sunshine.
He could not understand
why the ant never stopped to play.

## p.5

The ant lifted another seed
and carried it through the long grass.

"Why do you bother to work so hard?"
asked the grasshopper.
"Come and sing with me instead."
The ant paused for a moment
and looked up at him.
"I am storing food for winter," she explained.

## p.6

"Winter is far away," said the grasshopper.
"There is more than enough to eat!"
And besides, why should we worry
when the sun is warm and bright?

## p.7

"You should prepare while food is plentiful,"
answered the ant.
"Cold days will come sooner than you think."
The grasshopper shook his head.
"You are just wasting your time!"
he laughed.
He leaped away to sing
among the flowers.

## p.8

Day after day, the ant worked.
She carried seeds, grains, and crumbs
into her little home.
The grasshopper played music
from morning until night.
Whenever she passed, he invited her to rest.
But the ant kept preparing
for the coming winter.

## p.10

Summer faded, and autumn arrived.
The green leaves turned gold and brown.
The ant knew that it was time
to prepare for the coming winter.
She filled her home with seeds
and made it warm and safe.

## p.12

Winter arrived with a bitter wind.
Snow covered the meadow
and hid every plant from sight.
The grasshopper searched everywhere for food.
But he found only a few seeds left.
"I'm freezing out here in the meadow,"
he cried. "And I'm starving, too!"
He remembered the busy ant
and hurried to her home.

## p.14

He knocked softly on the ant's door.
The ant opened it and saw him shivering.
"Please share some food with me,"
begged the grasshopper.
"I'm sorry, but I have only enough
for those who prepared," said the ant.
The grasshopper lowered his head
and knew she was right.

## p.15

The ant gave him a small meal
so he would not go hungry.
"Next summer, I will work beside you,"
promised the grasshopper.
They ate the seeds
and slept through the winter.

## p.16

The grasshopper crawled away,
knowing how foolish he had been.
And by the time the snow had disappeared,
he was a much wiser creature indeed.
