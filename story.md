## p.3

Jed was a pilot at a big airport.
He flew the work planes.
They were kept in the big sheds by the runway.

## p.5

One day, Jed got a call
from a man at the airport.
“Please come now in your little plane.
We need you to help us.”

Jed started up
the engine.

## p.7

The little plane went slowly
down the runway
and stopped by the big planes.

The big planes looked down
at the little work plane and laughed.
“You will have to get
out of our way,” they said.
“We are going to take off soon.”

## p.8

A man came running
over to the little plane.
He gave Jed two big bags.

“Take these bags up to the farm
on the big hill,” said the man.
“There is so much snow up there
that the farmer can’t get out.
He needs this food for his family.”

## p.9

Jed pulled the bags of food
into the little plane,
and it moved slowly
down the runway.

## p.11

The little plane went faster and faster.
Then away it went up into the sky.

It went out over all the houses
and the farms.

Soon it came to the big hills.
There was snow all over them.

“I hope I can find the farm,”
said Jed.

## p.13

The little plane flew
around the hills again and again.
Jed looked for the farmhouse.
Then he saw it.

The farmer was outside.
He was waving to the little plane.

“That looks like the place,”
shouted Jed.

## p.15

Jed pushed the bags of food out.
They fell down
into the snow by the farmer.

“Good work!” shouted the farmer.

And the little plane
flew back to the airport.

## p.16

The little plane went slowly
past the big planes.

“I did a very good job today,”
said the little plane.
“I helped someone who needed me.”
